# 0.11.1 cancellation decision ownership

Status: released 0.11.1.

## One Watchdog cancellation decision

`TimerRegistry::cancel` owns Watchdog cancellation directly. One match selects
the callbacks to clear: a scheduled declaration clears its wakeup, dispatched
work clears both wakeup and work, and running or inactive declarations emit no
immediate cleanup. A selected cleanup also owns the immediate state change and
cancellation accounting, eliminating the separate pre-state accounting predicate.
The single-caller `cancel_watchdog` helper and boolean
`CallbacksToClear::wakeup_and_maybe_work` constructor are deleted.

Running cancellation still queues `Cancel` unless `Unregister` is already
pending. Inactive cancellation preserves its reason and counts nothing. Lifetime
removal still follows the final execution state; cancelling a fresh transient
declaration removes it, while running work retains its declaration until normal
completion. Ordinary and Watchdog effects share one normal transition construction
after the policy-specific decisions, since cancellation cannot exhaust generations.
Claim validation, generation history, provider detachment, cleanup ordering,
completion arbitration and unexpected provider failure handling are unchanged.

This private behavior-preserving simplification fits the next patch, 0.11.1.
Public APIs and snapshots are unchanged; there is no storage, binding generator,
downstream adapter or compatibility path to update.

## Verification and delivery scope

fixture now cancels after requesting unregistration. It checks no immediate effect
or cancellation accounting and still requires completion to clear the successor
and remove the declaration. The maximum-generation cancellation fixture uses
explicit expected callback sets after deletion of the boolean constructor.

- `fresh_remove_when_stopped_cancellation_releases_every_policy`.

- `watchdog_completion_arbitrates_immediate_cancellation_and_unregistration`.
- `watchdog_terminal_cancellation_makes_queued_callbacks_stale`.
- Runtime `watchdog_cancellation_clears_successor_and_queued_work` and
  `replacement_and_cancellation_clear_actual_owned_handles`.

provider heap bounds; no new recovery or performance claim is made.
