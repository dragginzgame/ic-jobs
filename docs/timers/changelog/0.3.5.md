# 0.3.5 liveness observation and IcyDB adoption record

Status: released 0.3.5.

## Claim-scoped liveness

- `OnceRegistration`, `AfterCompletionRegistration`, and
  `WatchdogRegistration` expose `has_armed_wakeup()`.
- The result is derived from the exact registration claim's canonical provider
  handle. It is not inferred from a snapshot, persisted authority, or a
  promise that the provider callback will execute.
- A watchdog's separately queued work callback does not count. Its pre-armed
  cadence successor does.
- Consumers still call the idempotent ensure operation unconditionally when
  their durable authority requires a wake-up; the observation must not become
  a check-then-arm control path.

## Downstream record

- The maintained IcyDB record captures its accepted 0.226.1 candidate hard
  cut to exact `ic-timers` 0.3.4, one shared registry, removal of the direct
  provider path, and its real-canister watchdog evidence.
- IcyDB has not yet adopted this 0.3.5 API. Its generated watchdog currently
  derives liveness from an inert snapshot and can switch directly to the
  claim-scoped observation after this release is available.
- Canic adoption remains pending.

## Validation

- Focused native tests cover inactive, armed, cancelled, pre-armed watchdog,
  and expired transient-claim observations.
- PocketIC does not need to be repeated because this change reads existing
  canonical handle ownership and does not alter provider binding or the
  scheduler/work protocol.
